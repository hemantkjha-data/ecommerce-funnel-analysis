The dominant leak is view → cart (−80.9%): the product page loses 4x more shoppers than checkout does. Fix priority #1: product-page relevance, price visibility and social proof — not payment flow.

Conversion by Device

Mobile carries 35% of traffic but converts at half of desktop
Desktop 5.77% vs Mobile 2.76%. Mobile cart→checkout drop is 49.2% — guest checkout, wallets and address autofill are the targeted mobile fixes.

Conversion by Traffic Source

Channel quality, not just volume
Social is the biggest volume but worst converter (1.49%); Direct 7.02% and Email 6.76% convert 4-5x better — shift paid spend toward retention channels and intent targeting.

Source × Stage — Where Each Channel Leaks

Sessions reaching each stage, by traffic source
Social leaks hardest at checkout (67% cart→checkout drop) — low purchase intent. Email users skip browsing (22% cart intent, highest) — ideal for cart-recovery triggers at the 55% cart→checkout drop.
